# HVCG — Lead Magnet Webpage: Product One-Pager

*Describes the module as it works for a business owner today.
## What it is

HVCG is where a business owner creates a lead magnet (a hook) — a single webpage designed
to trade something valuable (a checklist, a guide, a discount) for a visitor's
name, phone, and email. That lead magnet is used later to attract and
capture leads. This module is only about creating that page and reviewing it
before it goes live — nothing about ads, notifications, or what happens to a
lead afterward is part of this module.

## Two ways to start

1. **"I have a webpage"** — the owner already has a page live somewhere and
   just wants to use it as their hook. They paste the address, and it's live
   immediately — no build step, no review. HVCG is simply recording a link the
   owner already vouches for.
2. **"I need to create one"** — the owner doesn't have a page yet, so HVCG
   builds one for them: a **Give-Away Webpage** — teaser copy the owner writes
   plus a file they upload (a guide, checklist, or similar), gated behind a
   short capture form. A visitor fills in their name, phone, and email to
   unlock the download; that submission is a real, qualified lead.

## The walkthrough

After signing in, the owner picks one of the two paths above. Choosing "I need
to create one" drops them straight into the Give-Away Webpage form: teaser
title and description, the file to give away, and a business name/location.

Two AI helpers sit alongside the form, **both off by default**:
- **Pre-fill from a source** — point at an existing webpage or upload a
  document and matching fields fill in automatically; everything stays
  editable before submitting.
- **Pull my logo/tagline onto the page** — lifts a logo and tagline from that
  same source and applies them to the page's look.

Neither runs unless the owner turns it on, and either can be used without the
other. An owner who doesn't have a give-away file ready yet can browse a
gallery of real examples (filtered by industry) for inspiration before
uploading their own.

Before submitting, the owner also picks a **look** for the page — not just a layout
(split, banner, overlay, or stacked) but a real webpage design template complete with color palette, font & iamges.
5 templates in all. This isn't cosmetic-only: it's chosen right on the
creation form, before the page is even generated.

## Review, before anything goes live

Submitting a Give-Away Webpage form generates the page and lands the owner on
a **Review** screen — a live preview in a frame they can open full-size. From
here they can:
- **Approve** — the page goes live and starts accepting submissions.
- **Change the look** — switch to a different layout, or
  fine-tune font, background, and text color directly; a plain-language
  warning appears if a color choice would be hard to read on a phone in
  daylight. Each change regenerates the preview instantly.
- **Edit and save changes** — correct any field and regenerate before
  approving.

Nothing goes live from this path without the owner clicking Approve. If they
leave mid-flow and come back later, they land back where they left off —
Review if not yet approved, or the finished page if it's already live —
instead of starting over.

## After it's live

The owner gets a stable link to copy and share, plus a one-line confirmation
that the page is accepting submissions. They can return anytime to create
another hook, going through the same flow from the top.

## Deliberately out of scope for this module

- No verification that an "I have a webpage" link is genuine, safe, or live —
  the owner is vouching for their own page.
- No video or audio give-aways in this version — file-based give-aways only.
